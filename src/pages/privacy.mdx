This is the personal website of Abhishek Goyal, based in Gurugram, India. I want it to collect as little as
possible. This page says what that is today, and it will be updated **before** anything new is collected.

## What the site collects today

**No forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics.** Pages are static and nothing you do on them is recorded
by me.

- **Server logs.** The site is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse,
  Cloudflare processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested.
  I use these logs only to fix problems, and they are kept for a few days.
- **Your theme choice.** If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own
  browser's storage. It never leaves your device.
- **Email.** If you email me, I receive what you send through Google Workspace and use it only to reply.
  You can ask me to delete the conversation at any time.

## Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and Google Play take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

## What will change

Two things are planned. Neither is live yet, and this page will describe each one in full before it is:

- a **contact form**, so enquiries can reach me without email
- **privacy-respecting analytics**, only with your consent, to see which pages are useful

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and the GDPR if you are in the EU), you can ask what
I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Write to
[contact@abhishekgoyal.me](mailto:contact@abhishekgoyal.me) and I will reply as soon as I can.
