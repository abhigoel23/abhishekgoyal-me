This is the personal website of Abhishek Goyal, based in Gurugram, India. I want it to collect as little as
possible, and this page says exactly what that is. If anything changes, this page is updated **before** it
does.

## The short version

- Reading the site records nothing about you beyond what the host needs to serve pages.
- If you send an enquiry, I use it only to reply to you and follow up on that enquiry.
- Enquiries are deleted automatically 18 months after they arrive.
- Analytics run only if you accept them, and never receive your name, email or message.
- You can ask me to delete your data at any time: reply **DELETE** to my email, or write to
  [contact@abhishekgoyal.me](mailto:contact@abhishekgoyal.me).

## The contact form

**What it collects.** Your name and email, plus what you choose to tell me:

- for a project: company (optional), the kind of help you need, a budget range, a timeline and your message
- for a full-time role: company, role title, how the role is set up, a link to the job (optional) and your
  message (optional)

It also records the time you ticked the consent box, the page you first arrived on, and any campaign tags
in that page's address (for example `utm_source=linkedin`) or the site that linked you here. Those help me
understand which posts and pages lead to real conversations. If you have accepted analytics, the form also
sends the anonymous Google Analytics ID for your visit (see below). The site does not store your IP
address.

**Why.** To reply to your enquiry and follow up about it. You give consent when you tick the box, and you
can withdraw it at any time by asking me to delete your data. I never sell your data, add you to a mailing
list, or use it for anything else.

**Spam protection.** To keep bots out, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks signals from your
browser to tell people from bots, and a per-IP limit on how often it can be submitted.

## Booking a call

If you book an intro call, you do that on Cal.com, which has its own
[privacy policy](https://cal.com/privacy). Cal.com sends me the booking's time, your name and your email,
and I keep them with your enquiry.

| **Cloudflare**       | Hosts the site, stores enquiries and bookings, runs Turnstile, and counts visits without cookies             |
| **Google Workspace** | My email, and a Google Sheet that holds a working copy of enquiries and bookings                             |

Some of these companies store data outside India, for example in the United States or the EU.

## How long it is kept

Enquiries and bookings are **deleted automatically 18 months** after they arrive, from both the database
and my Google Sheet. The confirmation and notification emails stay in the mailboxes until they are deleted.
Delivery services such as Resend keep their own sending logs for a limited time under their own policies. If
you ask me to delete your data, I remove it from the database, the Sheet and my inbox.

## Analytics and cookies

- **Google Analytics.** A bar at the bottom of the page asks first. Nothing from Google loads until you
  press **Accept**, and pressing **Decline** (or ignoring the bar) means nothing is sent. If you accept,
  Google Analytics sets two cookies, `_ga` and `_ga_<id>`, that expire after 6 months. It counts pages
  viewed and clicks on buttons such as "Start a project", and never receives your name, email or message.
  When an enquiry is saved, the site tells Google Analytics that an enquiry happened, with only its type,
  budget range and where the visit came from. Ad features and Google signals are switched off.
- **Changing your mind.** Use **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page. Declining after you accepted
  deletes the Google Analytics cookies. Your choice is saved in your browser's storage for 6 months, then
  the bar asks again.
- **Visit counts without cookies.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without
  identifying you, so I can see roughly how many people visit even when analytics are declined.
- **Your theme choice.** Saved in your browser's storage if you switch between light and dark mode. It never
  leaves your device.
- **Where you arrived from.** The page you landed on and any campaign tags are kept in your browser's
  session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab. They are sent only if you submit the form.

## Server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, Cloudflare processes
technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. I use these logs only
to fix problems, and they are kept for a few days.

## Email

If you email me directly, I receive what you send through Google Workspace and use it only to reply. You can
ask me to delete the conversation at any time.

## Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Play and Cal.com take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and the GDPR if you are in the EU), you can ask what
I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Reply **DELETE** to my confirmation email, or write to
[contact@abhishekgoyal.me](mailto:contact@abhishekgoyal.me). I will act on it within 30 days, and usually
much sooner.

This site is not meant for children under 18, and I don't knowingly collect their data.
