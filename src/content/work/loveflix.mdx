## The brief

A clickable mock-up of a micro-drama streaming app, built solo as a coded prototype. I built eight screens
pixel-close from a provided design system: the Home feed, Series Detail, a coin store, My Library, the
Series Player, Login, the Paywall and a vertical Discover feed.

It's written in **TypeScript** on **React Native with Expo SDK 58**, with **expo-router** for navigation,
and it runs on iOS, Android and the web.

## Real video, in a vertical player

Playback is real, not a placeholder. Each episode is a slice of a streamed Creative Commons film, played
with **expo-video** in a full-screen vertical player.

## The cliffhanger and the paywall

The next episode locks right after the cliffhanger ("To be continued…"), and a paywall follows. Four ways
to unlock it work in the prototype:

- **Coins**, with a top-up from the coin store
- **A season bundle**
- **A VIP subscription**
- **A 5-second rewarded ad**

All four share one in-app wallet state, so an unlock shows everywhere in the app at once.
