## The job

I built 100% of the Android client, from concept to production release: **30+ screens** across candidate
and recruiter flows. The client is under NDA, so its name and screens aren't shown here.

## An architecture that outlived the contract

A modular **Compose + MVVM + Hilt + Flow** codebase. The app still runs on it.

## Video responses, end to end

- **Lifecycle-safe CameraX capture** of candidates' video responses
- **Upload** of candidate responses
- **Media3 / ExoPlayer playback** for recruiters' screening feeds
- **On-device speech transcription** with offline caching
