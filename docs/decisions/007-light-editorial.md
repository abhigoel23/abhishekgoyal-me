# 007 — Light editorial layout for B2B buyers

**Status:** accepted · 2026-11-27 · supersedes the palette and homepage layout in [005](005-brand-direction.md)

The site is now a storefront for a mobile engineering practice. Its buyers are Indian startups and US/EU product teams, who need to see proof, engagement terms and credentials quickly. The Studio direction (ADR 005) already had the right fonts, the resume's `#1F4E79` blue as its only accent, and 4px corners, so those stay. What changes is the light palette and the homepage layout. The page is a warm off-white `#FAF9F6`. Cards and surfaces are pure white `#FFFFFF`, separated from the page by 1px `#DEDBD2` lines rather than shadows. Ink is slate `#0F172A` and muted text is `#475569`. Light is the default. Dark mode stays for people who choose it, either with the toggle or through the OS preference, and keeps its ADR 005 palette, where cards sit one step above the page as they do in light. The homepage hero is left-aligned, with an inline-SVG offline-first sync diagram instead of the photo, which moves to `/about`. Below the hero, the page reads as an editorial list of evidence: a full-width flagship case study, a second case study, short NDA-safe outcome cards (role · domain · outcome), engagement models as a text table, a numbered process and the credentials. The layout avoids icon grids, gradients, glassmorphism, glows and centered heroes, which read as template marketing to this audience. The trade-off is that white cards on an off-white page have low contrast (1.04:1), so the 1px line does the separating work and must never be removed from a card. `src/lib/theme.test.ts` still enforces WCAG AA for every text pair in both themes.
